# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without gatekeeping.
- Respect privacy, licenses, attribution, and project boundaries.
- Accept maintainers' moderation decisions and help de-escalate conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban
repeat or severe offenders.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private advisory channel](https://github.com/ZJUZhiyuCai/vsdx-trace/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
